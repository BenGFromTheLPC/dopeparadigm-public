# Shake-It!

## What It Is

**Shake-It!** is a shipped game project and a strong example of using Codex as a creative building partner to move from idea to real App Store product.

## Role in the Ecosystem

Shake-It! proves that the Dope Paradigm world is not limited to music and admin systems. It also includes playful, public-facing creative work that can ship as a standalone product.

## Audience

- game players
- casual public users
- people exploring the broader creative range of the ecosystem

## Why It Matters

Shake-It! is important publicly because it shows:
- product execution
- experimentation
- range
- the ability to ship with AI-assisted workflows without losing authorial direction

## Status

Live and important as public proof that this system is capable of producing real shipped products, not just internal infrastructure.
