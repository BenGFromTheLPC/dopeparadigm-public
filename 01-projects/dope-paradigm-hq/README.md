# Dope Paradigm HQ

## What It Is

**Dope Paradigm HQ** is the public-facing iOS app in the Dope Paradigm world. It is designed as the fan-facing home for music, content, and deeper artist access.

## Role in the Ecosystem

Dope Paradigm HQ is where the public experience becomes real. It turns the internal catalog and publishing work happening elsewhere in the system into something fans can actually browse, play, and connect with.

## Audience

- fans
- supporters
- music listeners
- future community members inside the Dope Paradigm world

## Platform

- iOS

## Current Focus

Current work has been centered around:
- live music catalog browsing
- Apple Music playback
- cleaner guest vs signed-in behavior
- stronger account/security flows
- better catalog visuals and metadata
- more reliable music discovery surfaces like Latest Release and Top Plays

## Recent Milestones

- Apple Music connection and in-app streaming are live
- guest browse flow was cleaned up
- imported artwork now shows throughout the app
- Top Plays now uses a server-backed play counting system
- sorting and release metadata were improved across Albums, Singles, and All Tracks

## Framework / Feature Notes

At a high level, Dope Paradigm HQ currently leans on:
- SwiftUI
- Firebase Auth
- Firestore
- Firebase Functions
- Apple Music / MusicKit

## Status

Active and evolving.

Dope Paradigm HQ is one of the clearest examples of the Dope Paradigm ecosystem becoming a real product surface instead of just an internal idea.
