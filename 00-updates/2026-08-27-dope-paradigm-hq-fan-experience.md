# 2026-08-27 — Dope Paradigm HQ: Building the Fan Experience

Dope Paradigm HQ continues to move from an early music app concept toward a complete fan-facing product for the Ben-G From The LPC creative world.

## Project

- Dope Paradigm HQ
- Related system: Dope Paradigm Portal
- Platform: iOS

## What Changed

- Refined guest browsing across albums, tracks, and release surfaces.
- Improved the transition from guest browsing into sign-in and account creation.
- Strengthened account creation, profile editing, display names, and validation.
- Refined Apple Music playback through MusicKit.
- Improved catalog sorting, release metadata, and artwork behavior.
- Continued developing Top Plays with server-backed play counting.
- Added clearer separation between the public fan experience and internal operator tools.

## Why It Matters

A fan app has to feel like more than a collection of screens. It needs to make discovering music, playing it, creating an account, and returning for more feel natural. This work strengthened the product loop around Dope Paradigm HQ while giving the larger ecosystem a clearer public-facing home.
